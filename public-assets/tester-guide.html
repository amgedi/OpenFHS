FIRST TESTER ALPHA

Help us make OpenFHS easier to use.

This is an early prototype, so we are testing the basics before anything bigger. Please use invented cats and events only. It is not a diagnostic tool, monitored care service, or research study.

Open the practice diary →

Try a few things

  1. Create an invented cat and record an episode. Try “unknown” or leave an optional answer blank.
  2. Start a daily check-in, leave midway, reload, and continue the draft. Only submitting should complete the calendar bar.
  3. Open Records & exports and correct an entry. Check that the reason and original answers remain available.
  4. Download a readable report and a complete backup. Import the backup as separate copies; existing cats should remain.
  5. Try your phone, keyboard, zoom, reduced motion, and preferred language. Report anything hard to understand or reach.

How to give feedback

Use Help & support in the diary to choose a topic and app area, then review and download your report or open an email draft for openfhs@gmail.com. In-app delivery and automatic receipts are not connected in this public alpha. Nothing is submitted to a research database.

Tell us what you tried, what you expected, and what actually happened. Confusing wording counts as useful feedback too. Include the app version and device/browser when you can. Remove personal information from screenshots and do not send private backups or videos.

For researchers and veterinary reviewers

We welcome feedback on question wording, uncertainty and missing-data categories, observation coverage, correction history, and useful export formats. Please distinguish usability suggestions from scientific validation. We are not recruiting study participants or asking for patient records.

Known limits

  • Browser storage can be lost. Keep a downloaded backup. Each browser and website address has separate storage.
  • Translations are incomplete drafts. Consent, research wording, and other content still need fluent and expert review.
  • A limited security review has been completed, while storage-failure and accessibility coverage remain incomplete. Neither that review nor automated tests are a security certification.
  • No cloud account, research upload, model training, or automatic support sending is available here.

Privacy on a hosted website

Diary entries and clips remain in your browser unless you explicitly export or share them. The hosting provider still receives ordinary web requests, such as your IP address and requested website files, and may retain access/security logs under its policies. No analytics or advertising scripts are included. Support emails are separate and contain what you choose to send.

Anyone who has the link can view this prototype. Search-engine exclusion is a request, not password protection.
