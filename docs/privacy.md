# Current alpha privacy boundaries

This is a fictional-data usability alpha, not a research study. Do not enter real medical information, private records, identifying household details or private videos.

Diary records and drafts use browser storage. Clips use IndexedDB on the same device. Storage belongs to the browser profile and app address; another browser, private window, hosted address or offline launcher has separate storage. Clearing browser data may erase entries. Anyone with access to the browser profile may read them.

The application includes no analytics or advertising scripts. The static tester package disables support sending and outbound app connections. External support/donation links open separate services whose policies apply. A future host receives ordinary requests, including IP addresses; local-first does not mean a host sees no connection metadata.

Complete downloaded backups include local videos and drafts and are **not encrypted**. Ordinary JSON/CSV/readable exports do not include video bytes. Keep backups and original media privately. Import creates separate copies and resets effective sharing preferences; it does not replace the existing cats.

Research, model-training and public-sharing settings are independent **future-use preferences**, initially off. They do not upload data, enroll anyone, grant access to researchers, or publish a dataset. No participant infrastructure is present. The project commits not to sell data; this alpha does not collect a central diary dataset.

Software licensing does not grant rights to owners' records or media. Commercial data-use policy and future research governance require separate decisions. The AGPLv3 software license permits commercial software use.

No security, privacy, anonymization, regulatory-compliance or permanent-storage guarantee is claimed. Report suspected privacy/security problems privately to openfhs@gmail.com using fictional reproduction steps. Do not send private backups.
